# FFmpeg experiment media attribution

These three clips are adapted from Blender Foundation open movies and are
distributed under the [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
The clips are video-only, 60-second excerpts of the original movies from
300 to 360 seconds. They were scaled and re-encoded as H.264 for decoder
testing. The adaptations do not imply endorsement by the original creators.

| File | Source and attribution | Changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bbb_720p_h264_60s.mp4` | [Big Buck Bunny, Sunflower edition](https://download.blender.org/demo/movies/BBB/), Blender Foundation / Big Buck Bunny | Cropped in time, audio removed, scaled to 1280x720 at 30 fps, encoded as H.264 High. |
| `tos_720p_h264_60s.mp4` | [Tears of Steel](https://download.blender.org/demo/movies/ToS/tears_of_steel_720p.mov), Blender Foundation / Mango Open Movie Project | Cropped in time, audio removed, prepared at 1280x720 and 30 fps as H.264. |
| `sintel_1080p_h264_60s.mp4` | [Sintel](https://download.blender.org/durian/movies/Sintel.2010.1080p.mkv), Blender Foundation / Durian Open Movie Project | Cropped in time, audio removed, scaled from 1920x818 to 1920x1080 with vertical stretch, kept at 24 fps, encoded as H.264 Main with CAVLC. |
