    [llvm-profgen] Add build-id identity checks for IP sample mode
    
    Validate executable mapping evidence, buildid-list input and separate
    debug-file identity before counting addresses. Reject pseudo-probe builds
    and conflicting or ambiguous identity without changing the LBR path.
    
    Treat generation zero as unknown for unverified mappings: perf synthesizes
    zero for pre-existing mappings. Different devices or inodes and unequal
    nonzero generations remain ambiguous. Inode reuse may be undetectable when
    one generation is unknown.
    
    Cover identity grades, missing evidence, debug chains, probe rejection and
    generation ambiguity with lit tests and full recorded-input replays.
